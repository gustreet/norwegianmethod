# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Runners using the Norwegian Singles Method who export Garmin TCX files and want session feedback that matches Copeland’s sub-threshold rules—not Garmin’s VO2max labels.

## Product Purpose

Turn one track workout into a rep-by-rep verdict: controlled sub-threshold work, recovery discipline, and heart-rate drift versus an estimated LTHR.

## Brand Personality

Direct, coach-like, data-first. No hype.

## Anti-references

Garmin’s generic “execution score,” hero metrics, and VO2max primary-benefit labeling when the session was meant to stay sub-threshold.

## Design Principles

1. Judge the method, not the watch’s auto labels.
2. Show every rep; hide nothing in averages.
3. One screen answers “was this a good Norwegian single?”
4. Upload TCX locally; no account required.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, reduced-motion safe charts.
